On day one the station knows the sky.

A fresh install shares only daylight and the weather. Giulia can call the sunset, nothing more. The other moments on this page happen after you open the door: you choose what the hosts may use, entity by entity, and until you do, they talk about the weather like every other station on earth. In the real product, nothing counts as having aired until a listener actually received the audio: the hosts’ memory, the home-moment receipts, even the health check all wait for that. A radio station that only believes its own broadcast once somebody heard it.
