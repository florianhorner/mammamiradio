# Social Meme Style — Signal Contrast

The established Mamma Mi Radio meme language is **maximal before/after**, not a
generic webcomic. Its source examples live outside the repository in the local
Downloads archive: one contrasts a flat AI transition with an exclaim-mode
reaction, the other contrasts a nameless radio with a fully realised station.

## Format: same thing, completely different feeling

Use a dramatic, one-glance split screen. The left side is competent but grey,
generic, and emotionally flat. The right side is gloriously overcommitted:
Italian colour, music, warmth, a host reaction, and absurdly specific life.

The narrative grammar is:

1. a familiar, sensible default on the left;
2. a sharp visual seam;
3. the Mamma Mi Radio version on the right;
4. a final line that names the emotional upgrade.

The default format is 16:9. A 4:5 adaptation retains the black headline and
payoff bars, with the split screen remaining the dominant composition.

## Visual language

- **Headline bars:** black, full width, condensed uppercase white display type.
  The top states the comparison; the bottom lands the payoff.
- **The divide:** an unmistakable electric-blue and sun-gold lightning seam.
- **Left:** monochrome or desaturated, quiet, minimal, intentionally ordinary.
- **Right:** saturated Italian maximalism — tricolour confetti, gold radio,
  warm room light, music notes, and a fictional host celebrating the moment.
- **Cards:** one or two white, black-outlined speech/sign cards may carry a
  precise joke; the right-side card can be starburst-shaped.
- **Palette:** the existing espresso `#14110F`, cream `#F5EDD8`, sun gold
  `#F4D048`, Lancia red `#B82C20`, and Italian tricolour accents.

## Copy and safety

- Use the recurring construction: `SAME <THING>. COMPLETELY DIFFERENT <VALUE>.`
  or `YOUR <THING>. IT HAS A <SOUL / REACTION / HISTORY>.`
- Lead with the contrast, not technical explanation. The right side earns the
  product's personality through visual excess.
- A personal-lore joke may say what a host *implies*, but must not show raw Home
  Assistant entities, addresses, schedules, or real-world private facts.
- Use fictional hosts and original characters. Do not reuse celebrity,
  politician, film, or template imagery from a source meme.

## Production rules

- Render original art without text, then add deterministic high-contrast
  overlays. The composition must read muted at mobile-feed scale.
- The station's world is delighted and theatrical, never creepy or dystopian.
- Preserve the established high-energy split-screen language; reserve calm,
  editorial cards for product explainers and showreel proof.
